About this prototype

NS AI VIDEO STUDIO is an early mobile-friendly prototype for the nubia Neo 3 5G. It uses browser-based canvas animation and local recording for basic image motion.

True text-to-video and image-to-video AI require a compatible model and substantial processing power. This prototype does not promise unlimited AI generation, native 4K generation, or MP4 export on every browser.

For best results, use a clear image, close other apps, keep the phone cool and allow enough free storage before exporting.
